Home Care Protocol

The ten days after.

Protect your investment, preserve color brilliance, and keep hair feeling silky at home.

Protocol 01

48-Hour Color Shield

Avoid washing your hair for 48 hours after any chemical or color service to allow the newly formed color molecules and lipid bonds to fully stabilize within the cortex.

Protocol 02

Sulfate-Free Cleansing

Always use color-safe, sulfate-free shampoos and cool-to-lukewarm water. Harsh surfactants and scorching hot water strip protective cuticles and accelerate pigment fading.

Protocol 03

Thermal Heat Discipline

Always mist a professional thermal protectant spray before using flat irons, curling wands, or high-heat blow dryers. Never exceed 350°F on highlighted or lightened strands.

Protocol 04

Weekly Botanical Mask

Replace your regular conditioner once a week with a deep-conditioning bond repair or moisture masque. Leave in for 10 minutes to maintain elasticity and prevent split ends.

Real Questions

The questions people actually ask.

Honest answers to the most common styling, color, and maintenance questions our stylists receive every day.

How do I choose between half foils, full foils, and balayage?

Half foils are ideal if you want bright accents around your face and parting while maintaining natural depth beneath. Full foils provide all-over maximum lightness. Balayage offers a softer, hand-painted grow-out with no harsh demarcation lines, perfect for guests who want 3–4 months between touch-ups.

Will coloring or highlighting damage my fine hair?

We formulate with professional Matrix bond-protecting systems that minimize structural stress during lightening. Combined with our customized processing times and post-color acidic gloss treatments, your hair maintains its natural elasticity, softness, and moisture.

What should I bring to my bridal or formal styling appointment?

Please arrive with clean, completely dry hair (washed the evening before is ideal). Bring your veil, hairpins, tiara, or accessories, along with reference photos of styles you love. We also recommend wearing a button-down shirt so you can change into your attire without disturbing your style.

How often should I schedule a haircut to grow my hair longer?

To grow healthy length without losing shape, we recommend a "micro-trim" (dusting the ends by 1/4 inch) every 8 to 10 weeks. This eliminates split ends before they travel up the hair shaft, preserving total density and thickness.
