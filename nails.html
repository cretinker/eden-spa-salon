Nail Care Protocol

The ten days after.

Keep your cuticles hydrated, prevent hangnails, and maximize polish longevity.

Protocol 01

Nightly Cuticle Oil

Apply a single drop of jojoba or vitamin E cuticle oil to each nail bed every evening. This keeps gel pliable and prevents peeling at the edges.

Protocol 02

Gloves for Cleaning

Wear rubber gloves when washing dishes or using household disinfectants. Harsh detergents dehydrate natural nail keratin and weaken polish bonds.

Protocol 03

Never Peel Gel Polish

Peeling off gel forcibly removes microscopic layers of your natural nail plate. If a corner lifts, book a 15-minute gentle professional soak-off.

Protocol 04

Daily Heel Moisture

Apply a thick urea or shea butter cream to your heels after showering to preserve the baby-soft smoothness achieved during your pedicure.

Real Questions

The questions people actually ask.

Honest guidance on sanitation standards, dry times, and footwear recommendations.

How do you sanitize metal nail tools and pedicure foot baths?

Your safety is our highest priority. All metal instruments are sealed in surgical pouches and sterilized in a medical-grade autoclave. Our pedicure foot spas are 100% pipe-free to prevent any hidden bacterial circulation, and are sanitized with hospital-grade disinfectant between every single guest.

How long does regular polish take to dry compared to gel?

Gel polish is cured instantly under LED light — it is 100% hard, dry, and smudge-proof the second you stand up. Regular polish is dry to the touch in 15 minutes, but takes about 1 hour to cure to core hardness.

What should I wear to my pedicure appointment?

We suggest wearing loose pants or shorts that roll easily above the knee for the calf massage and hot towel wrap. If you choose regular polish (non-gel), please wear or bring open-toed sandals so your polish isn't smudged on the drive home.

Can I get a pedicure if I have ticklish feet or sensitive nails?

Absolutely! Our nail technicians use firm, grounding contact techniques rather than light, ticklish brushing, which eliminates ticklish sensations. Just let your technician know your preferences.
