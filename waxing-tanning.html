Preparation & Care

How to Prepare for Best Results

Waxing Preparation

  • Allow hair to grow for 2–3 weeks (approx. grain of rice length).
  • Gently exfoliate 24 hours prior to prevent ingrown hairs.
  • Avoid heavy caffeine right before your appointment to minimize skin sensitivity.
  • Wear loose, comfortable cotton clothing.

Spray Tanning Preparation

  • Shower, shave, and exfoliate thoroughly 8–12 hours before your tan.
  • Do not apply lotions, deodorant, perfumes, or makeup on the day of your session.
  • Wear dark, loose-fitting clothing and flip-flops to your appointment.
  • Moisturize daily after your first rinse to extend your tan for 10+ days.
