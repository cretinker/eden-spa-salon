Aftercare Protocol

The ten days after.

Preserve your sunless tan and keep newly waxed skin calm and bump-free.

Protocol 01

6 to 8 Hour First Rinse

For spray tans, take your first rinse 6 to 8 hours post-session using only lukewarm water without soap. Pat skin dry gently with a towel; do not rub.

Protocol 02

Loose, Dark Clothing

Wear loose-fitting dark clothing and avoid tight waistbands or underwires immediately after spray tanning to avoid friction marks.

Protocol 03

No Hot Showers for 24h

Following waxing, avoid hot tubs, saunas, heavy sweating, and swimming pools for 24 hours to prevent bacteria from entering open hair follicles.

Protocol 04

Daily Oil-Free Hydration

Moisturize your entire body twice daily with an oil-free, gentle lotion. Hydrated skin holds spray tan DHA 4 days longer and prevents dry flaking.

The questions people actually ask.

Honest guidance on hair length requirements, tan preparation, and sensitive skin precautions.

How long does my hair need to be before a waxing appointment?

Hair should be approximately 1/4 inch long (about the length of a grain of rice), which typically equals 2 to 3 weeks of growth after shaving. If hair is too short, the wax cannot grip properly; if it is longer, your esthetician will trim it to the ideal length before starting.

Will the spray tan look unnatural or turn orange?

Never. We use premium organic beet-derived DHA with natural violet and olive color correctors that neutralize any brassiness or orange tones. Your technician custom-mixes the level to match your natural undertones for an authentic, sun-kissed finish.

Can I get facial waxing if I use retinol or acne prescriptions?

Please discontinue using topical Retin-A, retinol, glycolic acid, or salicylic acid on the target area for at least 5–7 days prior to waxing. If you are taking oral Accutane (isotretinoin), waxing cannot be performed until 6 months after completing the medication; we can tweeze instead!

How does Ear Candling work and what does it feel like?

Ear candling is a relaxing, gentle experience. A hollow beeswax cylinder is placed lightly at the entrance of the ear canal and lit at the opposite end. The subtle warmth and gentle chimney vacuum create a soothing crackling sound, helping loosen excess wax and relieve sinus pressure.
