Skin Care Protocol

The ten days after.

Protect your refreshed skin barrier, prevent sensitivity, and extend your facial glow.

Protocol 01

Pause Retinoids for 72 Hours

Give active prescription retinoids, high-strength AHA/BHA acids, and physical scrubs a 3-day rest to allow the newly resurfaced skin barrier to stabilize.

Protocol 02

Daily SPF 30+ Shield

Freshly exfoliated skin is highly photosensitive. Apply broad-spectrum mineral or chemical sunscreen every single morning, rain or shine.

Protocol 03

Clean Brushes & Pillowcases

Wash your makeup brushes and sleep on a fresh pillowcase tonight. This keeps freshly cleared pores protected from transient environmental bacteria.

Protocol 04

Ceramide & Water Sealing

Focus on hydrating botanical toners and ceramide-rich moisturizers to lock in deep trans-epidermal moisture and preserve cellular bounce.

Real Questions

The questions people actually ask.

Honest, transparent guidance on skin reactions, extraction safety, and facial preparation.

Book Facial Online

Will my skin break out or turn red after extractions?

We perform manual extractions only on fully prepared, steamed follicles with ultrasonic assistance to avoid capillary trauma. You may have minor temporary pinkness for 1–2 hours, but our calming post-extraction botanical masks ensure you leave glowing and presentable.

How do I choose the right facial if I have reactive, sensitive skin?

You don't need to guess! Every facial begins with a thorough Wood's lamp diagnostic consultation. If your skin exhibits barrier compromise or redness, your esthetician will automatically pivot to gentle enzyme exfoliation and our CBD calming protocol.

Can I apply makeup immediately after my facial?

We recommend letting your skin breathe without foundation or powder for the rest of the day to allow active serums and treatment oils to penetrate fully. If you must apply makeup, mineral-based cosmetics are preferred.

Are your skincare products safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

Yes! Please let us know during intake. We substitute any high-percentage salicylic acid or chemical peel ingredients with pregnancy-safe lactic acid, fruit enzymes, and soothing botanicals.
