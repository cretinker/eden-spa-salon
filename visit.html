Preparation Guide

The week beforehand.

Simple guidelines to prepare your skin, body, and mindset so you receive the deepest therapeutic results from your appointment.

Days 7 – 4
Hydration & Skin Rest

Drink plenty of water. If you have a facial booked, pause aggressive AHA/BHA chemical peels, retinols, and excessive sun exposure to ensure skin barrier comfort.

Days 3 – 2
Waxing & Tan Prep

For body waxing, allow hair growth of ~1/4 inch (2 weeks from shaving). For spray tanning, gently exfoliate skin 24 hours prior and avoid applying heavy oil-based lotions.

Day of Service
Comfortable Attire

Wear loose-fitting, soft clothing. Arrive with clean skin and minimal jewelry. Have a light meal or healthy snack beforehand, avoiding heavy meals right before massages.

Immediately Before
15m Arrival Window

Arrive 10 to 15 minutes before your scheduled start time. Park freely in our front lot, switch your phone to silent mode, and allow your body to transition into sanctuary mode.
