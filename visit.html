Sanctuary Guidelines

Spa & Salon Etiquette

To ensure every guest enjoys peace, please review our standard policies before arriving.

Arrival Time

Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment. This ensures ample time to check in, complete any health intake forms, change comfortably, and unwind in our quiet lounge with herbal tea.

24-Hour Cancellation Policy

We understand schedules change. If you need to reschedule or cancel, please provide at least 24 hours' advance notice so we may offer that reservation window to clients on our waiting list.

Cell Phone Quiet Zone

To protect the serenity of all guests, we kindly request that all mobile phones and electronic devices be switched to silent or vibrate mode upon entry.

Health & Medical Disclosures

Please inform your therapist or stylist of any medical conditions, pregnancy, allergies, recent surgeries, or skin medications (such as Retin-A or Accutane) prior to treatment.

Gratuity Etiquette

Gratuities are at your discretion in appreciation of outstanding service. Standard spa gratuity ranges from 15% to 20% and can be added conveniently at checkout.
