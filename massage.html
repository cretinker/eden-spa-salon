Post-Care Protocol

The ten days after.

Maximize the physical benefits of your session and support optimal muscle recovery.

Protocol 01

Intense Hydration Protocol

Drink at least 32–48 ounces of fresh water in the 6 hours following your massage. Rehydrating muscle fibers assists the kidneys in flushing metabolic byproducts.

Protocol 02

Avoid Heavy Workouts

Allow your body 24 hours to recover before engaging in high-impact weight training or strenuous cardio. Gentle walking or light yoga is highly encouraged.

Protocol 03

Warm Epsom Salt Bath

Soak in a warm bath with 2 cups of Epsom salts the evening of your massage. The transdermal magnesium absorption relaxes tight muscles and eases minor soreness.

Protocol 04

Postural Awareness

Be conscious of phone and desk posture over the next several days. Keep monitor at eye level and perform gentle neck and chest opening stretches every 2 hours.

Real Questions

The questions people actually ask.

Clear, honest guidance on modesty, pressure preferences, and what to expect in the treatment room.

What should I wear, and how is modesty protected?

You will disrobe in total privacy to your comfort level (many guests disrobe completely, while others prefer to keep undergarments on). You will lie under a soft sheet and warm blanket. Your therapist practices professional draping at all times, only uncovering the specific arm, leg, or back section being actively treated.

Will deep tissue massage hurt during or after the session?

Deep tissue should feel like a satisfying, therapeutic release — never sharp, agonizing pain. We constantly calibrate to your feedback. You may experience mild muscular awareness (similar to post-exercise soreness) for 24–48 hours as restricted fascia recovers.

Can I receive a massage if I am currently pregnant?

Yes! We offer specialized Prenatal Nurturing Massages for guests in their second or third trimester (13+ weeks). We utilize specialized side-lying bolster systems to ensure total safety and comfort for both mother and baby.

How frequently should I schedule massage therapy?

For acute pain or chronic posture knots, a session every 2 to 3 weeks produces rapid results. For general stress relief and preventative wellness, a monthly session is ideal to maintain loose fascia and relaxed nervous system balance.
